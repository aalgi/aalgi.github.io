About PM SHRI

PM SHRI is a centrally sponsored scheme of the Government of India intended to develop more than 14,500 exemplar schools where students feel welcomed and cared for, with safe and stimulating learning environments, wide learning experiences and appropriate infrastructure.

The scheme is designed to showcase the implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 and to share learning and best practices with other schools.

“PM SHRI schools shall set examples for the best in class in the country.”
