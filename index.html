About the school

PM SHRI A.A. Latif Girls' Institution

PM SHRI A.A. Latif Girls' Institution, Mankachar was established in 1962 in South Salmara-Mankachar district of Assam. Located in Mankachar Block, the school is situated in a rural area and is committed to imparting quality education to female students from Classes I–XII.

School Profile

Block: Mankachar

PIN: 783131

Affiliation: Board of Secondary Education, Assam (SEBA)

Principal's Message

Education is a powerful force that moulds students into responsible and good citizens and helps transform the world for the better. At PM SHRI A.A. Latif Girls' Institution, we believe education is not merely about acquiring knowledge; it is about developing character, confidence, values and a sense of responsibility towards society.

Girls' education is especially important because educated girls become educated mothers, and educated mothers play a vital role in shaping the future generation. When mothers are educated, families become more aware, children receive better guidance, and the foundation of an educated and progressive nation becomes stronger.

About PM SHRI

PM SHRI is a centrally sponsored scheme of the Government of India intended to develop more than 14,500 exemplar schools where students feel welcomed and cared for, with safe and stimulating learning environments, wide learning experiences and appropriate infrastructure.

The scheme is designed to showcase the implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 and to share learning and best practices with other schools.

“PM SHRI schools shall set examples for the best in class in the country.”

Academics

Learning at our school

The school provides education from Classes I to XII. Academic provision includes primary, middle, secondary and higher-secondary education, with Arts stream at the higher-secondary level.

📚

Classes I–V

Primary-level education with a focus on foundational learning and overall development.

🧪

Classes VI–VIII

Middle-level education supporting continued academic learning and student development.

📝

Classes IX–X

Electives: Garment Designing, Tourism, Retail, Advanced Mathematics, Hindi and Geography.

🎓

Classes XI–XII

Stream: Arts only. The school is affiliated to the Board of Secondary Education, Assam (SEBA), as confirmed for this website.

Vision & Mission

Empowering girls through education.

Our Vision

To empower girls through quality education, knowledge, skills and values, enabling them to become confident, responsible and capable individuals who can contribute positively to their families, communities and the nation.

Our Mission

  • To provide quality and inclusive education to girls from the local community.
  • To develop students' knowledge, skills, confidence and character.
  • To encourage students to become responsible and good citizens.
  • To provide opportunities for academic, creative, cultural and practical skills.
  • To promote girls' education and empowerment as an important foundation for progress.
  • To nurture learning, discipline, cooperation and social responsibility.
