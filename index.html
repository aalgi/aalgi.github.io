PM SHRI

Schools for Rising India

The PM SHRI scheme aims to develop exemplar schools that provide welcoming, safe and stimulating learning environments, strong infrastructure and a wide range of learning experiences.

The initiative is aligned with the National Education Policy 2020 and emphasizes holistic development, inclusion, skills, sports, arts, ICT, sustainability and citizenship.

“PM SHRI schools shall set examples for the best in class in the country.”

— PM SHRI vision statement
