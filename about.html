About the school

About PM SHRI A.A. Latif Girls' Institution

PM SHRI A.A. Latif Girls' Institution, Mankachar was established in 1962 in South Salmara-Mankachar district of Assam. Located in Mankachar Block, PIN 783131, in a rural area, the school is committed to imparting quality education to female students from Classes I to XII.

School Profile

Established: 1962
District: South Salmara-Mankachar
Block: Mankachar
PIN: 783131
Area: Rural
Students: Female students
Classes: I–XII
Affiliation: SEBA (Board of Secondary Education, Assam)

Principal's Message

Education is a powerful force that moulds students into responsible and good citizens and helps transform the world for the better. At PM SHRI A.A. Latif Girls’ Institution, we believe education is not merely about acquiring knowledge; it is about developing character, confidence, values and a sense of responsibility towards society.

Girls’ education is especially important because educated girls become educated mothers, and educated mothers play a vital role in shaping the future generation. When mothers are educated, families become more aware, children receive better guidance, and the foundation of an educated and progressive nation becomes stronger.

Our endeavour is to provide our students with quality education and an environment where every girl can learn, grow, dream and contribute meaningfully to society.

IH
Irshad HassanPrincipal
Vision & Mission

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To empower girls through quality education, knowledge, skills and values, enabling them to become confident, responsible and capable individuals who can contribute positively to their families, communities and the nation.

Our Mission

  • To provide quality and inclusive education to girls from the local community.
  • To develop students' knowledge, skills, confidence and character.
  • To encourage students to become responsible and good citizens.
  • To provide opportunities for academic, creative, cultural and practical skills.
  • To promote girls' education and empowerment.
  • To nurture learning, discipline, cooperation and social responsibility.
